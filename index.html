🦏 Kaziranga National Park

❓ Exam Connection

Which national park is famous for the one-horned rhinoceros?

🗺️ 1. Actual Location

📍 Kaziranga National Park
Assam, India

🌊 Brahmaputra River

The park lies in the Brahmaputra Valley floodplain.

📸 2. Real Image

🦏 One-horned rhinoceros — one of Kaziranga's most important wildlife species.

🌿 3. Environment

Kaziranga's landscape includes floodplain grasslands, wetlands, shallow pools and woodland areas.

📜 5. Historical Background

The Kaziranga Proposed Reserve Forest was created in 1905. It later became a national park under the Assam National Park Act of 1968.

🌍 UNESCO World Heritage Site: 1985

⭐ 6. Why Is Kaziranga Important?

  • Important habitat for the one-horned rhinoceros.
  • Major wildlife conservation area.
  • Important Brahmaputra floodplain ecosystem.
  • Supports many mammals and bird species.
  • Recognized as a UNESCO World Heritage Site.
